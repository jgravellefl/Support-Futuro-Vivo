Who We Are

Transforming Lives in the Dominican Republic

Support Futuro Vivo is a U.S.-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to providing critical resources to Futuro Vivo School in Guerra, Dominican Republic — a school serving disadvantaged children for over 30 years.

Founded by two Sisters from Spain, the school gives children who would otherwise have nothing the chance to learn, grow, and thrive. We fund free schooling, comprehensive medical care, daily meals, and transportation for every student.

100% of every donation goes directly to the school. No administrative overhead. Every dollar changes a child's life.

What We Provide

Comprehensive Support for Every Child

Our programs remove every barrier standing between a child and a quality education.

Program 1

Comprehensive Healthcare

Many of the children we serve would go without any healthcare if not for the school. Futuro Vivo provides on-site pediatricians, psychologists, nurses, and social workers — giving each child the physical and emotional support they need to thrive.

Early intervention through school-based healthcare has a proven impact on long-term academic and life outcomes, and it is a cornerstone of everything we do.

Program 2

Safe Transportation

Distance is a real barrier to education in Guerra. We fund safe, reliable transportation so that every enrolled child — regardless of where they live — can attend school consistently.

By removing this logistical obstacle, we ensure that attendance is never a function of geography, and that every child has an equal opportunity to show up and succeed.

Program 3

Daily Meals

With generous support from the McCann Family Foundation, every student at Futuro Vivo receives a nutritious meal during the school day. For many, this is the most reliable meal they will have.

Proper nutrition is inseparable from a child's ability to focus, learn, and grow. Our meal program ensures no student sits in class hungry.
